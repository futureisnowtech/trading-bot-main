2. **DATE BOUNDARY**: Scope historical trade reads, learning, and live execution metrics to `TRADE_DATA_START_DATE = "2026-07-23"`. Broker exposure and open-position reconciliation remain unbounded for safety.
3. **NO PAPER LANES**: Paper lanes A and B are retired; `forecast_positions_paper*` are dropped. Do not reintroduce a parallel simulated book.
4. **ONE PnL FORMULA**: Realized PnL comes only from `runtime/kalshi_settlement_truth.settlement_pnl_usd`
   (`winning_contracts − yes_total_cost − no_total_cost − fee`, rounded to cents). Never re-derive it
   inline. Never treat `max(yes_cost, no_cost) − min(yes_cost, no_cost)` as exit proceeds: both fields
   are costs, so that form is non-negative by construction and scores a win on every two-sided row —
   it once reported +$346.11 / 88.3% on an account whose true result was −$6.60 / 61.3%. Never trust
   `revenue` either; the API returns 0 for it on the large majority of live rows.
5. **DROPLET-FIRST ANALYSIS RULE**: When the user asks about live performance, recent trades, archived probabilities, Jarvis/Cerebro/RBI evidence, win-rate by tier, or anything phrased like "our system" rather than "this local file", inspect the droplet first. ## Production Architecture (v19.20.3, release lineage 2026-08-25)

- **Production Probability Path**: Entry probability uses keyless deterministic GFS and ECMWF forecasts with explicit model/horizon forecast-error sigma, promoted RBI GFS/ECMWF weights, actual NCEP AIGFS disagreement as an uncertainty scaler, and an optional near-term HRRR splice for daily-HIGH. Bounded high/low temperature physics is applied in degrees Fahrenheit before the contract CDF; no temperature correction is injected into rain, snow, wind, or hourly-temperature probabilities. The current `bounded_heuristic_v1` coefficients are operationally proven but not yet outcome-validated, so their skill claim remains pending the new RBI epoch. Commercial Open-Meteo ensembles and ICON are absent.
- **RBI Learning Gate**: `RBI_LEARNING_EPOCH=v19.20.0-deterministic-physics-path` excludes evidence generated by older probability contracts. Every market that reaches canonical pricing retains its point-in-time model trace even when a later gate vetoes trading; pre-pricing failures remain unscored. RBI remains on its governed 60/40 GFS/ECMWF baseline until at least seven days and 24 independent, officially settled current-epoch events exist; challenger validation and human promotion are separate mandatory gates.
- **Provider Activation Boundary**: Production ingestion is the keyless deterministic Open-Meteo GFS/ECMWF/AIGFS route plus HRRR, METAR, and official settlement observations. It fails closed when the canonical GFS record is absent and never relabels a surviving provider. Do not add a commercial ensemble endpoint or API-key branch without a new governed design and proof epoch.
- **Convergence Guardrail**: The 1.5x multiplier is earned only when every available physical model agrees above 75% or below 25%. A maximum pairwise probability gap above 20 points shrinks `q_hat` toward 50% and sizes down to a 0.60x floor; above 70 points is a hard entry veto.
- **Station Calculus at the 5-Minute Decision Gate**: Each fresh METAR observation updates a time-normalized first-order thermal derivative; every decision cycle locks out daily-HIGH YES entries after 14:00 local when dT/dt <= -0.20F/hr. The derivative expires after 90 minutes and is ignored unless its age is explicit and fresh.
- **Value Price Bracket Gate ($0.20 - $0.70)**: Restricts daily HIGH/LOW entries to the versioned value zone, with a separate held-side minimum-price gate deleting penny longshots.
- **$2.0°F Safety Buffer**: Enforces minimum model projection headroom to prevent boundary miss wipeouts.
- **No Cheat-Code or Goldmine Lane**: The dead `find_cheatcode_underpriced_contracts()` and city-priority marketing helpers were removed. Live selections come only from the canonical pricing, economics, weather, firewall, and risk gates.
- **Live Enforced Risk Posture**: Source fallbacks match NYC effective policy: 90% max deployed, 20 concurrent positions, 5 per event family, max 15 contracts and $10 base exposure per position, quarter-Kelly sizing, and regional exposure `max($20, 40% of live cash)`. Conviction tiers may raise the dollar cap according to the explicit multipliers in `config.py`. Covariance uses empirical correlation only after every requested station coordinate has 90 raw local-calendar days from the keyless Open-Meteo historical archive; this is not NOAA/ASOS observation truth. Until then, a gross comonotonic no-netting upper bound binds.
- **Enforced Probability-to-Size Controls**: Convergence, divergence, predictive sigma, AIGFS uncertainty, and same-family penalties feed the taker-only solver. `KALSHI_KELLY_CAP=0.12` caps fee-inclusive order capital and `KALSHI_MAX_RISK_PER_EVENT_PCT=0.08` caps aggregate family exposure before broker submission. Every qualified candidate refreshes broker positions before sequential risk admission. Covariance failures veto instead of failing open; the soft marginal multiplier cannot enlarge or erase a singleton admitted by the hard integer rails.
- **Versioned City Firewall**: The source fallback blocks ABQ, CHS, CLT, DC, DET, LV, MCI, MCO, MKE, MSP, OMA, PDX, PHL, PHX, RDU, SLC, and STL (17 cities), leaving CHI, DEN, LAX, OKC, SAT, MIA, ATL, BOS, DAL, MSY, AUS, NY, SF, SEA, and HOU (15 hubs) enabled. An explicit `CITY_BLACKLIST` env value remains an intentional override.
- **Governed Loss Response**: The former narrative-only “autonomous leak forensics” module was removed; it fabricated a boundary diagnosis and rewrote obsolete weights after every loss. Real losses enter official settlement truth, the daily firewall, RBI evidence, and governed champion review instead.
